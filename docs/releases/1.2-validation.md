# Itch comment follow-up — Collection v1.2

Reports reviewed on 2026-10-27:
- https://itch.io/post/17726048: platform/shadow colours and continued stutter.
- v1.1 devlog: no additional bug reports visible at review time.

## Changes

Background conversion now chooses the better of the two existing background
CRAM lines per tile, mapping each source colour to its nearest retained colour.
Previously, whole source palettes were assigned one line and colour merging
could retain an unnecessarily distant ancestor. Sprite/HUD palettes, opaque
pixel coverage and runtime palette allocation are unchanged. This reduces
conversion error; it does not restore every PCE colour or settle subjective
platform/shadow preferences.

Horizontal scrolling decodes each four-tile metatile column once, instead of
repeating the live map lookup for each of its tiles. The existing row
optimization, wrap handling, priority and transfer schedule remain intact.

## Evidence

- Background oracle: 1,744,896 pixel checks across all seven stages, including
  unused metatiles. All opacity and palette-ownership checks pass; no tile's
  weighted RGB333 error increases. Stage-one world-weighted colour error falls
  from 148,222 to 133,964 (9.6%). Other stages improve or remain equal.
- Streamer: 99,864,666 independent visible-cell comparisons pass with address
  and undefined-behaviour sanitizers, including both directions, diagonal
  motion, world wrap, plane wrap and large camera jumps.
- Matched stage-one diagnostic workload, Genesis Plus GX at 59.922743404312 Hz:
  7,353 shared completed ticks match camera, player, RNG and object counts.
  In ticks 1001–6000 (4,999 updates), refreshes fall from 5,002 to 5,000;
  prepared pictures rise from 4,980 to 4,982. Worst rolling 60-refresh count
  rises from 51 to 52; longest preparation gap remains four refreshes.
  The sampled map-streaming section averages 22.93 vs 18.96 scanlines on
  streaming passes. Both ROMs use identical profiling/input flags; this is
  subsystem headroom evidence, not a claim of hitch-free gameplay or scanout FPS.
- The fixture uses simulation-indexed input and life refill; it is not a natural
  whole-game playthrough. Candidate includes the new background art. Source
  baseline, ROM manifests, traces and discarded experiments are retained under
  `out/itch-review/`.

The sprite-call and audio-cache experiments were discarded because their timing
benefit was negligible. The release does not change the audio driver.

Physical consoles/flashcarts, PAL and the reporter's exact route remain untested.
The broad stutter report remains open; the improvement here is deliberately
limited to measured map-streaming work. The alternate translation and outside
colour-editing offer have not been accepted or incorporated.
