# SonSon Collection v1.3 — intro palette and arcade sound events

SonSon II now restores the stage sprite palette when you skip the intro. The
intro colours previously remained active during gameplay, making moving logs
purple and yellow and affecting other sprites. The original brown log artwork
and black outline now render correctly without replacing the assets.

SonSon I now translates gameplay sound events through the arcade's original
command table. Food pickups play their short effect instead of the extra-life
jingle; shots, enemy defeats and other events reach their intended sounds.
Effects are 9 dB louder on the FM path. Stop also clears layered cues, and the
PSG noise channel is muted when the source disables noise.

Both games remain in one Collection ROM. The music still uses the existing
Genesis approximation of the arcade's AY sound chips; this update does not claim
identical timbre or physical-console validation.
