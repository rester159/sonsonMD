## SonSon I audio

The main CPU queues event selectors, then maps them through $63AB/$63DD before
writing the sound latch. Native gameplay had begun passing these selectors to
a replay table indexed by latch bytes. The original ROM tables independently
verify all 25 mappings. Examples: food 4 -> 2, fire 8 -> 11, defeat 12/13 -> 8.
Convenience wrappers now use selectors too, including round music 15 -> 16.

`tools/verify_sound_events.py` verifies the native table against both original
ROM tables. Its optional `--host` harness covers 1,024 dispatch/gate cases.
New host executables stalled at macOS dyld startup in this session; no host
runtime pass is claimed. The actual linked sound routines were exercised in
Genesis Plus GX instead, using `tools/hardware_regression/sonson_audio_main.c`.

The identical target fixture exercises pickup, fire, defeat, extra life, music,
stop, and 20 seconds of music with frequent shots/pickups. The resulting stereo
PCM has zero clipped samples before and after. At a 20-unit RMS threshold with
10 ms windows, the pickup's audible span falls from 3.19 seconds to 0.63 seconds.
After the overlap case, Stop previously left a cue audible for the rest of the
capture; it now settles below the threshold within 30 ms. The FM attenuation
floor changes from 24 to 12 TL units (+9 dB), without changing the instrument.
PSG noise is explicitly silenced when its source mixer disables noise.

Evidence, PCM and before/after fixture ROMs are local under
`out/feedback-fixes/`. Do not publish the source ROMs or fixture binaries in Git.
The Genesis PSG/FM synthesis remains an approximation; audible equivalence to
the arcade, PAL timing and physical Model 1/2/3 sound quality are not established
by these tests.
